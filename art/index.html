Key Art

Painted concept art for Hayn and the Wolfwood.

Art
  1. Key Art
  2. Views & Maps

Page 1 of 3

Semi-realistic painted concept art, in the quality of a modern fantasy RPG: real proportions and materials, a painterly finish, never cartoony and never a photograph.

The default light is late afternoon in early autumn: a warm low sun, long shadows, cool blue shade, chimney smoke and the first golden leaves in the beeches. Dawn mist and lantern-lit nights are the alternates.
