Boss

The Alpha

The wolf that taught the pack to come out of the trees.

“It's bigger than the others. Bigger than any wolf should be. Take antidotes.”

Captain Roland

The Alpha leads the pack from a den under a rock outcrop deep in the spruce. It is the first boss of the vertical slice: winnable alone at level 5 with any job, and noticeably easier with another player who can hold its attention or keep you healed.

Where
The wolf den, deep in the spruce wood
Level
5, elite
Size
2.2 m long, 1.1 m at the shoulder
Drops
Alpha fang, always; a rare item, sometimes

Three phases

100 – 60 %

The hunt

Bite combos, a Lunge along a line you can see coming, and Rend, a bleed that a healer can cleanse.

60 – 25 %

The howl

It howls for two grey wolves and grows stronger. Interrupt the howl, or kill the adds fast.

Below 25 %

Frenzy

Faster attacks and Savage Spin, a circle around it marked on the ground. Step out, then finish it.

The arena

The den

A clearing of trampled earth and pine needles, ringed by fallen trunks, with the cave mouth at the back. Bones and a torn traveller's pack near the entrance. One shaft of pale light.
