The Journey

From Dais to Gate

The whole vertical slice, stop by stop.

The vertical slice is one road, walked in eleven stops, and every stop teaches one new thing.

Levels
1 to 5 by the Alpha, 6–7 after the dungeon
Quests
17 main steps, one optional
Bosses
The Alpha, and the dungeon boss

Act 0

The Wayward Dais

Spawn at the floating stone, learn to move, look around and read the first signpost.

“The stone hums. The road leads north.”

Act 1

The Watch House

Report to Captain Roland under the town-hall arcade. Dialogue, the quest log and the tracker.

“Captain Roland has a task, and not enough people.”

Act 2

Choose your path

Visit the four guilds and choose one job. Your trainer hands you a weapon, armour and your first two abilities.

“Steel, bow, spring water or flame.”

Act 3

The training grounds

Target a dummy, use your abilities, defeat a sparring partner, and reach level 2.

“The dummy does not hit back. Yet.”

Act 4

The forest edge

Gather Silverleaf in the meadow outside the north gate, before any wolf shows up.

“Silverleaf grows where the birches thin out.”

Act 5

The pack

Grey wolves in packs of two and three, a wild boar that charges. Loot, experience and level 3.

“Grey shapes between the beeches.”

Act 6

Village life

Sell pelts, forge Wolfhide Bracers, brew Healing Draughts, and find the scout's satchel at the ruined watchtower.

“Pelts for iron, iron for bracers.”

Act 7

The Alpha

Dark wolves in the spruce, then the Alpha in its den: telegraphs, summoned wolves and a frenzy at the end.

“Bigger than the stories said.”

Act 8

The road east

Escort a merchant's cart out of the East Gate, then hold off two waves of ambushers.

“One merchant's cart, and one already overturned.”

Act 9

Beneath the hill

A wounded scout warns you about the hill. Three packs, a two-phase boss, and a way back out to the road.

“Old stone, and something older behind it.”

Act 10

The gates of Oakhaven

Follow the last stretch of road to Oakhaven's gate, where the vertical slice ends.

“The end of the vertical slice.”
