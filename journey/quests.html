The Journey

The Quest Chain

Every step of the main story, and what waits on the side.

Eighteen steps from the first signpost to the last gate. Names are working names.

StepQuestWhat you do
1The Wayward RoadWalk from the Dais to the South Gate and read the signpost.
2Report to the CaptainFind Captain Roland at the Watch House.
3A CallingVisit the guilds and choose your job.
4First LessonsTrain with your guild master and beat the sparring dummy.
5Field SuppliesGather six Silverleaf at the forest edge for Mira.
6Cull the PackDefeat six grey wolves in the Wolfwood.
7Pelts for the SmithBring Haldor five wolf pelts.
8Gear UpCraft Wolfhide Bracers and equip a forged weapon.
9Bitter RemedyBrew three Healing Draughts at Mira's table.
10The Old WatchtowerFind the scout's satchel at the ruined tower.
11Darker WoodsDefeat four dark wolves in the spruce wood.
12The AlphaEnter the wolf den and defeat the Alpha.
13Rest at the Wolf's RestSet your return point at the inn and eat.
14Road to OakhavenEscort the merchant's cart from the East Gate.
15Ambush!Defend the cart through two waves.
16The Wounded ScoutOptional: help the scout and hear his warning.
17Beneath the HillClear the dungeon and defeat its boss.
18The Gates of OakhavenFollow the road to the gate.
