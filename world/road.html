World

Road to Frankfurt

A cart to guard, an ambush, a dungeon under a hill, and a gate at the end.

Leaving Hayn

The road to the capital

Once the Alpha is dead, Captain Roland asks you to see a merchant safely to Frankfurt. The road is quiet for a while.

Along the road

  1. North Gate

    The big gate toward the capital. The merchant's cart waits here.

  2. Milestone and wayside shrine

    Gravel, two worn tracks with grass between them, tall beeches on either side.

  3. The stone bridge

    A small arch over a brook.

  4. The ambush point

    An overturned cart and scattered crates. Two waves to survive.

  5. The wounded scout

    He has seen what is under the hill.

  6. The dungeon entrance

    An overgrown stone archway into a hillside, carved with wolf heads.

  7. The gates of Frankfurt

    The end of the vertical slice.

Beneath the hill

The dungeon

An entrance hall, three rooms of enemies, a bridge, a round boss chamber thirty metres across, and a tunnel back out to the road. Best with other players. It is the barrow of a forgotten wolf-cult, and its dead still stand guard.
