The forest

Rings that grow darker

About a square kilometre of temperate Central European forest north and west of the town, laid out in rings. Near the wall it is sunny and safe; further in, the trees get older and taller and the wolves get bolder.

The map

Ten zones

Each zone has its own trees, its own light and its own job in the journey, so you always have a feeling for how deep you are.

Zone by zone
