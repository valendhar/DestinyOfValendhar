World

Valendhar

Border villages, old forests and the floating stones that bring travellers in.

Valendhar is a world of border villages, old forests and long roads between walled towns. Most people live by timber, field and trade, and magic is something you meet in a tower or at a shrine, not on the street.

Scattered across the land stand the Daises: weathered stone platforms ringed with faint blue glyphs, each with a rough megalith floating above it. Travellers arrive by them and leave by them. Nobody in Timberwatch remembers who built them.

Arrival

The Wayward Dais

A circular granite platform twelve metres across, its rim carved with glyphs that glow softly blue. Above its centre, a rough-hewn stone hangs two metres in the air, with thin blue cracks running through it. This is where every adventurer in Timberwatch first sets foot.

The region

A clearing in a sea of trees

Timberwatch sits in a clearing about 300 by 240 metres on a gentle south-facing slope. A stream comes out of the forest in the north-west, feeds a mill pond, and runs past the west wall. Wooded ridges rise to the north and west.
