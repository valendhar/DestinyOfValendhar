Valendhar

A medieval Germany that never was: old forests, walled towns and the floating stones that bring travellers in.

Valendhar is a medieval Germany that never was: the United German Kingdom, ruled from Frankfurt on the Main. Most people live by timber, field and trade, and magic is something you meet in a tower or at a shrine, not on the street.

Scattered across the land stand the Rune Portals: weathered stone platforms ringed with faint blue glyphs, each with a rough megalith floating above it. Travellers arrive by them and leave by them. Nobody in Hayn remembers who built them.

Arrival

The Rune Portal

A circular granite platform twelve metres across, its rim carved with glyphs that glow softly blue. Above its centre, a rough-hewn stone hangs two metres in the air, with thin blue cracks running through it. This is where every adventurer in Hayn first sets foot.

The region

Three-Oaks, the king's forest

South of the river Main lies Three-Oaks, a royal hunting forest. Its name is said to come from three ancient oaks. Oak and beech woods cover it, broken by wet brook meadows, reed-fringed ponds and wide marshes the foresters call the Bruch, where the ground stays soft all year. The king's foresters guard the game, and the hunting courts sit in the villages around the edge.

Hayn sits in a clearing about 300 by 240 metres on a gentle south-facing slope. A brook comes out of the forest in the north-west, feeds a mill pond, and runs past the west wall. Wooded ridges rise to the north and west, and the neighbouring town of Ysenburg lies beyond them.

The capital

Frankfurt on the Main

North of the forest the land opens onto the broad, fertile valley of the Main, with the blue line of the Taunus hills on the horizon. Frankfurt grew on a low hill at an old ford of the river. A long stone bridge, with mills built against it, crosses to Sachsenhausen on the south bank, a quarter of fishermen, gardens and orchards, and walls and towers ring both halves of the city. Above the steep roofs rise the cathedral tower and the king's hall.

Frankfurt is where the heroes truly learn their craft, in the great houses of their guilds, and where the real adventure begins. The vertical slice ends on the hill above it, at the Saxenhausen Watchtower, with the city in view.
