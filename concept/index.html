Concept Art

Concept Art

Design and modeling sheets for everything in the vertical slice.

The working drawings behind the game: every hero, townsperson, creature, building and prop, drawn before it is built.

People are drawn as polished, semi-realistic 3D game characters. The world, its buildings and its props are painted concept art. Many sheets show an object from several sides, because they guide the 3D models. Empty frames are sheets still to come.
