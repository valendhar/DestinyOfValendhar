Characters

Creatures

Realistic animals first. The Wolfwood is dangerous because it is real.

ACT-CR-01

Grey wolf

Packs of two or three that flank you, and give up the chase if you run far enough. Level 2–3.

ACT-CR-01D

Dark wolf

The deep-wood variant: packs of three, a harder bite and a howl that strengthens the pack. Level 4.

ACT-CR-02

The Alpha

Charcoal fur, a heavy mane, a torn ear. The first boss of the vertical slice. Level 5, elite.

CR-03

Wild boar

Alone, and charges in a straight line you can see coming. Level 3.

CR-04

Red deer

A stag and a hind. Harmless, and part of what makes the forest feel alive.

CR-08

Brown bear

Not in the vertical slice for certain. If it comes, it lives deep in the spruce.

Past the wolves, bandits wait on the road east, and the dead of an old wolf-cult guard the barrow beneath the hill.
