People of Hayn

A captain, four guild masters, a smith, and the town around them.

Characters
  1. People of Hayn
  2. Creatures
Page 1 of 2

Short, grounded and a little dry: the people who run Hayn and send you out of it.

Captain Roland

Captain of the watch

A tired, decent border captain with too much road to guard and too few people to guard it. He gives you your first task and most of the ones after it.

Town Hall & Watch House

“You can hold a weapon? Good. That puts you ahead of most of my watch.”

Captain Roland

Bron

Drill master · Paladin guild

Gruff, loud, and right more often than anyone likes. Bron has trained every guard in Hayn.

Barracks & Training Yard

“Shield up. Again.”

Bron

Hagen

Ranger master · Ranger guild

Quiet. Hagen knows the Wolfwood better than anyone alive, and was the first to notice the pack changing.

Hunters' Lodge

“They're not hungry. That's what worries me.”

Hagen

Mira

Herbalist · White Mage guild

Warm and practical, and a Shumadii, one of the elf-like people from another world. Mira patches up the town, sells herbs, and teaches healing at the old spring shrine in her garden.

Herbalist's House & Spring Shrine

“Sit. Drink this. Then tell me which wolf.”

Mira

Elwyn

The mage · Elementalist guild

A Shumadii, one of the elf-like people from another world, older than anyone in Hayn can remember. Curious and slightly distracted, with candles melted onto stacks of books. The red glow in the tower window at night is the only magic most townsfolk ever see.

Elwyn's Tower

“Ah, a student. Mind the books. And the floor. And that.”

Elwyn

Hermann

The smith

Hermann sells weapons and armour, buys pelts, and will teach anyone with five pelts and some iron how to make a pair of bracers.

The forge

“Pelts. Not scraps. Pelts.”

Hermann

Also in town

The innkeeper

Runs the Golden Acorn. Bed, food and a place to come back to.

The storekeeper

Potions, rope, lamps and bags, on the square.

The woodcutter

At the forest edge, with something to say about the wolves.

The merchant

Waiting at the Rune Portal for someone to see his cart to Frankfurt.

The wounded scout

Found on the road, past the ambush.

Townsfolk

Guards, vendors, a baker, a miller, children, a bard in the inn, and a dog that chases the cat.
