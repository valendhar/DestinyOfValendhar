Gameplay greyboxing, part 4

The Paladin's trial and quest markers

The first job now has to be earned. The Paladin guild sets a short chain of trial quests, one leading to the next, and finishing it is what teaches you the Paladin job. Along with it come quest markers above people's heads, so you can read the town at a glance: one marker shows who has a new quest for you, and a different one shows who is waiting for you to hand a finished quest in.

November 2026, 2½ min
