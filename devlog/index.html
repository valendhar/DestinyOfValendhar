DevLog

The project at its different stages, on video.

Short videos from the work itself, as the game takes shape. The newest comes first.

November 2026 · 2 minutes

Gameplay bootcamp: the first quest

A testing ground built next to the town, where each gameplay system is tried out on its own before it goes into the real world. This first greybox area tests quests: the player takes on a task, the objectives track each step, and the quest completes. Small and plain on purpose, so that what works here can later be placed in Hayn with confidence.

Watch on Loom

November 2026 · 2½ minutes

Greyboxing, part two

The greybox one step further, recorded just before the town's design changed: the first timber-framed buildings stand in the streets, simple trees mark the green, and the player walks through it all with the game's interface on screen. The next update brings the stone wall and the new gates.

Watch on Loom

November 2026 · 2½ minutes

Initial town greyboxing

The first walk through Hayn in Unreal Engine: simple blocks for every building, the streets, the wall and the gates, to check that the town has the right size and feels right before anything is modeled.
