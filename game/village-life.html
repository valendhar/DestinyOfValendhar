The Game

Village Life

Between the fights: vendors, crafting, potions and a warm bed.

The forge

Haldor buys pelts and sells steel

Haldor the smith sells weapons and armour, buys the wolf pelts you bring back, and teaches you to forge: five pelts and some iron make a pair of Wolfhide Bracers.

The herbalist

Silverleaf into Healing Draughts

The herbs you gather at the forest edge go onto Mira's alchemy table and come back as Healing Draughts. Wolfsbane makes antidotes, which you will want before the Alpha.

The inn

A bed and a hot meal at the Wolf's Rest

Set your return point at the inn, and eat before a hard fight: the innkeeper's food gives a short boost.
