Targeting

Pick your target, then act

Press Tab to cycle through enemies in front of you, use the nearest-target key, or click an enemy. Your target shows in a frame at the top of the screen with its health and whatever it is casting.

Abilities

A hotbar, not a button-masher

Auto-attack runs while you have a target. Your abilities sit on a hotbar of at least ten slots on the number keys, with a shared cooldown that gives each choice weight. New abilities arrive at levels 2, 3 and 5.

What keeps a fight readable

Warning

Ground telegraphs

Every special attack marks its area on the ground when the cast starts: a line, a cone or a circle.

Timing

Cast bars

You see what your target is casting and how long you have, so you can interrupt or step out.

Feedback

Damage numbers

Hits, heals and misses float above the target, so every ability tells you what it did.
