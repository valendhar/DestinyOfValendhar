Overview & pillars

The Game

A fantasy online MMO of jobs, guilds and readable fights, starting in one border village.

Destiny of Valendhar is a third-person fantasy online MMO set in the world of Valendhar, played by many players at the same time. You pick a job, join its guild, and fight with a hotbar of abilities against enemies you select with a target key, the way classic MMOs play.

This site shows the game's vertical slice, not the full game. A vertical slice is one small part built to finished quality first: here, one village, one forest, one road and one dungeon, from the first step to the last boss. It is the part being built now, and the measure for everything after it.

What the game stands on

Pillar 1

A grounded world

Valendhar looks semi-realistic: real proportions, honest materials, painterly light. The villages read like late-medieval Central Europe. Magic is rare in town and grows stronger out in the forest and at the old monuments.

Pillar 2

Jobs, not classes on a menu

You choose your job in the world, by walking into a guild and talking to its trainer. Each guild is a real building with its own banner, people and training ground.

Pillar 3

Combat you can read

A target frame, cast bars, damage numbers and ground markings for every special attack. You should always know what is about to hit you, and have a moment to move.

Pillar 4

A gradient of danger

Outward from the village, the land gets older, darker and more dangerous: meadow, woodcutters' clearings, beech forest, deep spruce, the wolf den. You always know roughly how far you have come.

Pillar 5

Better together

Every fight can be won alone, and every fight gets easier with other players. A tank who holds attention and a healer who keeps people standing change how the Alpha plays.

The loop

What you do, again and again, between the Dais and Oakhaven.

  1. Take a task

    From Captain Roland, a trainer or a villager with a problem.

  2. Head out

    Gather herbs at the forest edge, hunt wolves, find what was lost.

  3. Come back

    Turn in, level up, sell pelts, buy and craft better gear.

  4. Go further

    New abilities open deeper, darker parts of the forest.
